MineHeart PvP Guides

Everything you need to know about tier testing, points, and climbing the leaderboard.

Guide 01

How to Get Tier Tested on MineHeart

Connect to play.mineheart.net and join our 1k-member Discord server. Use /tiertest in the lobby to select your gamemode and region. Once matched with an official tester, you will duel in a standardized arena kit to determine your placement (Tier 5 up to Tier 1).

Guide 02

Minecraft PvP Tiers Explained: What HT1 to LT5 Mean

Every gamemode uses 5 main tiers (Tier 1 being the highest, Tier 5 being entry competitive). Each tier is split into High Tier (HT) and Low Tier (LT) subdivisions. For example, HT1 represents the top tier in that kit, followed by LT1, HT2, down to LT5.

Guide 03

MineHeart Points and Titles: How Overall Works

Your Overall score is the sum of your points across all 8 gamemodes: HT1 (60), LT1 (45), HT2 (30), LT2 (20), HT3 (10), LT3 (6), HT4 (4), LT4 (3), HT5 (2), and LT5 (1). Reaching point milestones unlocks titles like Combat Ace, Combat Master, and Combat Grandmaster.
